      How to film for good mocap
      • The whole body in frame, head to feet, with a little room around.
      • Camera at waist height, still (a tripod or a shelf).
      • Even light, no strong backlight; clothes that contrast with the background.
      • Start and end in a T-pose or standing still for a second.
      • One person in frame; avoid baggy clothes that hide the knees and elbows.
